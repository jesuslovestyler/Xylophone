Xylophone

Tap the wooden bars and make a joyful noise — drag a finger or mouse across them and let the music run like a mallet over the keys.

Tip: hold and slide across the bars for a glissando. Many bars can ring together — try a chord with two fingers.

Every sound is synthesized live on your device with the Web Audio API — no audio files, nothing recorded, and nothing sent anywhere.
